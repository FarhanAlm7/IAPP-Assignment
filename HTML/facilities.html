Training Facilities

Football Training Field

Our main training field is a full-size natural grass field located at the APU sport centre, used for weekly training and internal matches.

Cloth Changing Room

Members have access to clean changing rooms to change your clothes and equipments before and after training sessions.

Gym Room

The APU gym is available for members to build strength and fitness, supporting overall performance.

Equipment Storage Room

We keep all our training gear here — cones, mini goals, footballs, and team bibs. Everything is organized so players can grab what they need before training starts.

Medical Station

A calm and clean clinic, there are beds for players to lie down, a first aid cabinet stocked with bandages and ice packs, and a trained staff member always nearby. If you get injured during training, this is where we take care of you.

Recovery Area (Coming Soon)

We're planning to introduce hydrotherapy sessions as part of our recovery program, helping players ease muscle soreness after intense training.
