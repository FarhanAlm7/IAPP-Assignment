Our Mission & Vision

Mission

APU Kickers is committed to promoting physical fitness, teamwork, and sportsmanship among students through regular football training and friendly competition. Welcome to APU Football Club for players of all skill levels!

Vision

We're building APU's football community to bring students together through sports, healthy living, and representing our school with pride on the big stage!

Objective

  • Organize weekly training sessions to improve players' technical and physical skills
  • Practice teamwork and discipline through matches and competitions
  • Provide opportunities for both beginners and experienced players to grow
  • Represent APU in external football competitions
