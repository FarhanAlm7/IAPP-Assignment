Rules & Regulations

General Conduct

  1. Everyone must sign up through the official Join Us form before joining any session.
  2. Please arrive at least 10 minutes before training starts. If you're late, warm up on your own and don't keep the team waiting.
  3. Respect coaches, teammates, and opponents at all times.
  4. Wear proper football boots and sports gear. No slippers or jeans.

Match Rules

  1. Matches follow standard 11-a-side football rules based on FIFA regulations.
  2. Each match has two 45-minute halves with a 10-minute break in between.
  3. Be there 15 minutes before kickoff to warm up. Don't show up right on time, and don't be late.
  4. Listen to the referee during the match. No arguing on the spot.

Penalties

  1. Repeated absences without notice may result in suspension from matches.
  2. Unsportsmanlike behaviour, arguing, or fighting will be reviewed by the club committee. Serious cases can lead to a direct ban.
  3. If you damaged our equipment during training or matches, you'll have to pay for it.
  4. Three unexcused absences will get you a warning. You'll be suspended from club activities for serious cases.
